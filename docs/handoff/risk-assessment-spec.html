Components

Risk status

The risk on a Risk assessment field. Three signals, never colour alone: a fill, an icon and the word. Always a label - on the row, the chip bar, the placeholder, the outcome card and the finalise table. Never pressable: the row's action sits beside it.

Anatomy

A 24px inline pill, the same geometry and tokens as ui-pill.

Parts
  1. 1Container – 24 tall, 0 8px inline (spacing-2), radius 999, 1px rim inside. Gap 4 (spacing-1) between parts.
  2. 2Status icon – 14 × 14, inherits the ink. flag for Risk flagged, check_circle for No risk, help_outline for Action required. Never swapped: the glyph is part of the status.
  3. 3Word – 12/16, 600. The status word. On a required-actions chip it is the field name instead, and the status word moves to the accessible name and the title.
Why these colours and no othersThey are ui-pill's danger, success and warn tones with the subdued rims its dotted form wears. A status is the same red, green or amber it is in the case header and the cases table, so the Risk assessment adds no colour of its own. An outlined No risk and two hover mixes were tried and dropped for that reason.

Beside the row's action

Where the agent can assess, the action follows the pill in one 64px slot: Assess on a field still to assess, a pencil (Change assessment) on one already assessed. The pill is the same either way.

Do / Don't

Do

  • Use it wherever a status is read: the row, the chip bar, the placeholder, the outcome card, the finalise table.
  • Put the action beside it on a row - Assess or the pencil - never inside it.
  • Override the word on a chip to the field name; keep the status on the accessible name.

Don't

  • Make it a button or an input. The row has Assess / Change assessment; the form has radios.
  • Give No risk a fill at rest, or swap an icon for a different glyph.
  • Add a fourth status. Informational fields carry no pill at all.

Do / Don't

Do

  • Give every value line its own span. The data is one fact per line; one wrapped paragraph hides that.
  • Mark the open row the moment its form opens, from its action or from a placeholder, and clear it on save or cancel.
  • Show "Assessed by" only after an assessment. An unassessed field has nobody to name.

Don't

  • Make the row or the pill clickable. The action is the control; the row holds copyable text and its own info button.
  • Put a status on an informational field, or let one be opened.
  • Truncate values. They wrap; the tooltip is for the definition, not for hidden data.
